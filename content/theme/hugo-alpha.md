# Alpha — Minimal Hugo Theme for Blogs, and Content Hubs

Alpha is a shortcode-driven Hugo theme designed for content-first websites: long-form articles, multipart series, personal blogs, or even books.  
It's built for both seasoned developers and complete beginners who want a professional, distraction-free site without the complexity.

## Features

- **Beginner-Friendly:** Comes with extensive documentation and the unique **LiVa** framework to help you avoid common errors.
- **Shortcode-Powered Layouts:** Build your pages with modular components instead of being locked into rigid templates.
- **Minimalist Design:** A clean, typography-focused aesthetic keeps the focus on your content.
- **SEO & Social Sharing Ready:** Full support for Schema.org structured data and Open Graph meta tags.
- **Tailwind CSS Powered:** Easy to customize and extend for those who want to dive deeper.
