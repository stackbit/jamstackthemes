## Recto for Astro: an editorial blog for Astro and Sanity

Recto for Astro is a free, MIT-licensed blog template for Astro and Sanity. Posts and pages are edited in Sanity Studio with live preview in the Presentation tool, and every page is built ahead of time as a static file.

**View demo:** [https://recto-astro.themes.dev/](https://recto-astro.themes.dev/)

## Features

* Home page, archive with pagination, posts with a photo and reading time, about page
* Visual editing with Sanity's Presentation tool
* RSS feed and a newsletter form
* Light and dark mode
* Runs on its own demo content until you connect a Sanity project
* Tailwind CSS v4
