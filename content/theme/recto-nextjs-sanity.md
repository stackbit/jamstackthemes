## Recto: an editorial blog for Next.js and Sanity

Recto is a free, MIT-licensed blog template for Next.js and Sanity. Posts and pages are edited in Sanity Studio with live preview in the Presentation tool.

**View demo:** [https://recto.themes.dev/](https://recto.themes.dev/)

## Features

* Home page, archive with pagination, posts with a photo and reading time, about page
* Visual editing with Sanity's Presentation tool
* RSS feed and a newsletter form
* Light and dark mode
* Runs on its own demo content until you connect a Sanity project
* Tailwind CSS v4
